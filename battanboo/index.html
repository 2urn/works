The lines

A line-up is the lines and their speeds together. Pick a ready-made one, or change any line below and it becomes Custom: add lines with the button, then save it under a name.

Two things land on one square at once (two lines crossing, a line meeting a finger held there, or a line meeting an always-on switch): that is a meeting. It makes a bigger sound, and a ring flashes where it happened. More things meeting makes it bigger.

The dice

Past scenes

Each time a new scene is rolled, the one you leave is kept here as "Session name · number". Tap one to bring it back with its line-up and tempo.

The sounds

The air

Air blends between the plain notes (0%, off) and clouds of tiny grains of each colour's own sound, spread in time, pitch and place (100%). Texture picks how thick the cloud is. Space adds reverb: how much of the sound comes back from a long room. Morph blends the sets: pick a second set, and at half, half the notes sound from it.

Wander: chance at every pass

The marks

Each step rolls a die for every lane. A step has a favourite mark and a favourite voice; the favourite comes up with the chance set above and the rest share what is left. The picture stays where it is and the line only says when: left to right, top to bottom, a radar sweep, a ring widening or closing. Time and pitch are the line's two directions (across a ring is pitch for a radar, round the ring is pitch for a widening ring). Up to three more lines play the same picture against the first, each at its own speed: 3:2 means that line makes three passes while the first makes two, 2:1 is double speed. Finger: touch or drag over the cells to play them; hold to sustain, and press harder where your device senses pressure (otherwise the note swells the longer you hold). Several fingers play several notes. The record dot captures what you play by hand and loops it; press it again to overdub, the cross clears it. Pencil: tap a cell to paint it, tap again to change its mark. The arrows step through the scenes you have rolled; the right arrow rolls a new one at the end. Robot plays the lines. The switches beside the grid set each row and column: open circle plays as the lines pass, slashed is muted, filled is always on (it sounds on every step of the first line). Space plays, R rolls.
